Technical Social Network

Synthetic Social Networks focus on having AI agent mimics the structure and characteristics of real social networks, allowing researchers to study social behavior and information diffusion without privacy concerns. These networks can be created using various algorithms and models to simulate realistic interactions among users:

Evaluation of Human Digital Twins

What is Human Digital Twins

It's virtual representation (a proxy model) of a person, built from real or gathered data so it can simulate and predict how that individual may behave. For human health, one roadmap approach models it using a five-level process that starts with wearable sensors and then moves to data collection, analysis, and decision-making systems.

The Challenges behind the scenes

  • Data: Health and behavior data are often incomplete, inconsistent, or scattered across systems. A twin can become inaccurate or drift away from the real person if updates are missing or delayed.
  • Validation: It’s difficult to prove that a twin’s predictions will hold for a particular person, especially as their condition changes. Bad predictions could lead to harmful decisions.
  • Privacy and consent: A twin may combine highly sensitive data and generate new predictions about someone. It’s unclear how to manage ongoing consent, withdrawal, deletion, and future uses of the model.

Safety and Displacement

  • Safety: A twin can give misleading advice if its data are incomplete, outdated, or biased. In health or workplace settings, acting on a wrong prediction could harm someone. The system should be tested, its uncertainty made clear, and important decisions kept under human oversight.
  • Displacement: Twins used to optimize staffing or simulate workers could contribute to job changes or replacement, and may shift control from workers to employers. They can also make workers feel constantly monitored. A 2023 study describes using worker twins to monitor conditions and reschedule jobs, illustrating both the potential safety benefit and the need for safeguards around worker autonomy and how the data are used.
